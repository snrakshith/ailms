# Production reliability and responsible AI — quick reference

## Monitoring, drift, reliability and retraining
**Recall:** Yes; input distribution can change while the target relationship stays stable.

**Watch for:** Treating every detected shift as concept drift or retraining automatically without verification.

**Verify:** Alerts have actionable ownership, context and known false-positive limitations.

## Responsible AI, governance and security
**Recall:** Intended use, evaluation, limitations, data considerations and known risks.

**Watch for:** Assuming public data is always licensed for training or logging full user conversations.

**Verify:** Every risk has mitigation, owner and validation evidence.

## Cloud architecture, scaling and deployment trade-offs
**Recall:** They have different resource, latency and scaling characteristics.

**Watch for:** Overprovisioning GPUs, exposing indexes publicly or scaling without measurement.

**Verify:** Architecture includes identity, encryption, limits, monitoring and failure isolation.
