# Mathematics for AI — quick reference

## Linear algebra: vectors, matrices and dot products

**Recall:** When either vector has zero norm.

**Watch for:** Confusing elementwise multiplication with matrix multiplication or ignoring zero norms.

**Verify:** Scores match and output shape is (2,).

## Eigenvalues, SVD and projections

**Recall:** Variance captured by orthogonal components of centered data.

**Watch for:** PCA components depend on scaling and do not necessarily preserve predictive information.

**Verify:** Rank-2 error is no larger than rank-1 error.

## Probability, distributions and Bayes rule

**Recall:** The posterior combines the likelihood with the prior probability.

**Watch for:** Confusing P(test positive given condition) with P(condition given test positive).

**Verify:** Posterior is about 15.4%, not 90%.

## Statistics, sampling and confidence intervals

**Recall:** Under repeated valid sampling, the interval procedure covers the parameter about 95% of the time.

**Watch for:** Ignoring dependence, selection bias or nonrepresentative samples.

**Verify:** Explain why larger samples generally yield narrower intervals.

## Calculus, gradients and optimization

**Recall:** It propagates gradients through composed layers.

**Watch for:** A learning rate that is too large can overshoot or diverge.

**Verify:** Identify convergence, oscillation and divergence using loss values.
