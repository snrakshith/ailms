# Advanced learning and generative models — quick reference

## Transfer learning, embeddings and self-supervised learning
**Recall:** Learning from supervision constructed from the data itself rather than manual labels.

**Watch for:** Training too aggressively can destroy useful pretrained features; domain shift can limit transfer.

**Verify:** Log trainable parameters, data split and performance.

## Autoencoders, VAEs and GANs
**Recall:** The generator produces insufficiently diverse outputs despite plausible individual samples.

**Watch for:** A good reconstruction score does not guarantee useful generation; GANs may collapse modes.

**Verify:** Show inputs, reconstructions and failure examples.

## Diffusion and score-based generative models
**Recall:** It performs the diffusion process in a compressed representation rather than directly in pixels.

**Watch for:** Confusing training noising with inference denoising or assuming fewer steps always preserve quality.

**Verify:** Explain the trade-off between sampling compute and quality.

## Reinforcement learning and preference optimization
**Recall:** Optimizing a proxy can yield undesirable behavior if it differs from the intended goal.

**Watch for:** Reward hacking, biased preference data and conflating reward with real user value.

**Verify:** Show exploration/exploitation effects and limitations.
