# F02-L02 — Eigenvalues, SVD and projections: worked solution

## Problem
Reconstruct a matrix with ranks 1 and 2; calculate Frobenius error.

## Worked approach
Use `X=np.diag([4.,2.,1.]); u,s,vt=np.linalg.svd(X,full_matrices=False)`. For k=1,2 reconstruct `(u[:,:k]*s[:k])@vt[:k]`; compute `np.linalg.norm(X-Xk)`. Expected errors √5 and 1. Compare singular energy retained (16/21 and 20/21).

## Failure analysis
Applying PCA to uncentered data measures variation around the origin; center using train statistics when variance around the mean is the goal.

## Acceptance check
Rank-2 error is no larger than rank-1 error.
