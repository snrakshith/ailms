# F01-L03 — NumPy arrays, broadcasting and vectorization: worked solution

## Problem

Standardize a 3-column matrix including a constant column, preserving its shape.

## Worked approach

Use `X=np.array([[1,2,7],[3,4,7],[5,6,7]],float)`. Compute `mu=X.mean(0); s=X.std(0); z=(X-mu)/np.where(s==0,1,s)`. Assert z.shape==(3,3), first two column means are zero and stds one, last column all zero. Reuse mu and s on future data.

## Failure analysis

A zero variance feature produces NaN; using axis=1 instead of axis=0 normalizes samples, not features. Verify shape and axis explicitly.

## Acceptance check

Nonconstant columns have near-zero mean and unit variance; the constant column remains finite.
