# P02-L01 — Monitoring, drift, reliability and retraining: worked solution

## Problem

Create drift and service alerts, escalation thresholds and a rollback plan.

## Worked approach

Create baseline vs current feature distributions and score histogram; compute PSI or simple mean shift with stated bins and epsilon, monitor labeled MAE when available and p95 latency/errors. Define thresholds, sustained windows, owner, triage and rollback criteria; simulate a benign seasonal shift and alert review.

## Failure analysis

Automatically retraining on every drift alert may worsen performance; investigate data integrity, seasonality and actual quality first.

## Acceptance check

Alerts have actionable ownership, context and known false-positive limitations.

## Submission evidence

Include implementation or design artifact, test inputs and outputs, relevant dependency versions, and a short limitation statement. For external tools, report skipped integrations explicitly; a mock is not a live service test.

- Correct mechanism or design: 4
- Evidence against acceptance criteria: 3
