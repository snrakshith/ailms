# P01-L03 — CI/CD, continuous training and DevSecOps: worked solution

## Problem

Design CI for a model service and simulate a failed quality threshold.

## Worked approach

Define pipeline lint/unit→secret scan→SCA/SAST→train/evaluate→package/SBOM/image scan→staging DAST/contract→approval→deploy. Simulate score below threshold and assert promotion blocked. Store immutable artifact digest and rollback target; keep provider-specific credentials scoped and short-lived.

## Failure analysis

Treating retraining as automatic deployment can promote degraded models; require independent quality/safety gates and rollback.

## Acceptance check

Failed tests or quality gates block release; artifacts are immutable.

## Submission evidence

Include implementation or design artifact, test inputs and outputs, relevant dependency versions, and a short limitation statement. For external tools, report skipped integrations explicitly; a mock is not a live service test.

## Grading rubric (10 points)

- Correct mechanism or design: 4
- Evidence against acceptance criteria: 3
- Failure handling and boundary cases: 2
- Reproducibility and limitations: 1
