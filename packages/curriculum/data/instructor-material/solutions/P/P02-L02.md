# P02-L02 — Responsible AI, governance and security: worked solution

## Problem

Draft data inventory, model limitations, approval checkpoints and incident procedure.

## Worked approach

Create inventory of data sources, permissions, sensitive fields, retention, model intended/excluded uses and high-risk decisions. For each risk assign owner, mitigation, test/evidence, residual risk and approval gate; draft incident containment, notification assessment and rollback procedure. Use synthetic fixtures for security tests.

## Failure analysis

A checklist with no test evidence or owner cannot demonstrate effectiveness; require concrete evidence and signoff.

## Acceptance check

Every risk has mitigation, owner and validation evidence.

## Submission evidence

Include implementation or design artifact, test inputs and outputs, relevant dependency versions, and a short limitation statement. For external tools, report skipped integrations explicitly; a mock is not a live service test.

## Grading rubric (10 points)

- Correct mechanism or design: 4
- Evidence against acceptance criteria: 3
- Failure handling and boundary cases: 2
- Reproducibility and limitations: 1
