# G03-L04 — RAG evaluation and retrieval diagnostics: worked solution

## Problem
Create ten answerable and five unanswerable queries and report retrieval and answer metrics.

## Worked approach
Build 10 answerable queries with gold evidence IDs and 5 unanswerable cases. For each record retrieved IDs, recall@k, answer, citations and grounded/abstained labels. Compute answerable accuracy and unanswerable abstention separately; inspect errors by chunking, retrieval, reranking or generation stage.

## Failure analysis
A single average conceals no-answer failures; stratify by answerability and report denominators and per-query traces.

## Acceptance check
Show per-query evidence and abstention behavior, not just averages.

## Submission evidence
Include implementation or design artifact, test inputs and outputs, relevant dependency versions, and a short limitation statement. For external tools, report skipped integrations explicitly; a mock is not a live service test.

## Grading rubric (10 points)
- Correct mechanism or design: 4
- Evidence against acceptance criteria: 3
- Failure handling and boundary cases: 2
- Reproducibility and limitations: 1
