# D04-L02 — Autoencoders, VAEs and GANs: worked solution

## Problem
Compare reconstruction on normal and shifted samples; explain how VAE sampling differs.

## Worked approach
Train simple autoencoder on normal synthetic vectors; score reconstruction MSE on normal vs shifted holdout and inspect cases. For VAE calculate z=mu+exp(.5*logvar)*eps and KL=−.5 sum(1+logvar−mu²−exp(logvar)); explain how sampling differs. GAN comparison is conceptual unless trained.

## Failure analysis
Good reconstruction does not ensure useful generation or anomaly detection; validate shift cases and avoid interpreting reconstruction score as calibrated probability.
