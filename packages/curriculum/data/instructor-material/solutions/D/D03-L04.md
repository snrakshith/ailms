# D03-L04 — Encoder, decoder and encoder-decoder architectures: worked solution

## Problem
Diagram the three architectures and label attention connections and objectives.

## Worked approach
Draw encoder input→bidirectional self-attention→representations (classification/MLM); decoder tokens→causal self-attention→next-token logits; encoder-decoder source→encoder, target prefix→causal decoder with cross-attention→target logits. Label allowed attention directions and compare translation vs completion.

## Failure analysis
Allowing target positions to attend to future target tokens during training creates leakage; use causal target mask.
