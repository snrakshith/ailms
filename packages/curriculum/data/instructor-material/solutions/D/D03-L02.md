# D03-L02 — RNNs, LSTMs, GRUs and sequence modeling: worked solution

## Problem

Train a small GRU on synthetic sequences and compare to a naive baseline.

## Worked approach

Generate independent sequences of length 8 with target sign of sum or next value. Train GRU(batch_first=True), linear head on final valid state; compare mean/last-value naive baseline on unseen sequences. Record accuracy/MAE and inspect long sequences and noise.

## Failure analysis

Using padded last timestep instead of true sequence end corrupts representation; gather last valid state or pack sequences.

## Acceptance check

Evaluate on sequences not used in training and explain failure cases.
