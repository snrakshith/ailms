# D04-L03 — Diffusion and score-based generative models: worked solution

## Problem
Visualize a simple noising schedule and compare denoising errors across timesteps.

## Worked approach
Choose x0 and fixed epsilon, beta schedule from .001 to .02, alpha_bar=cumprod(1-beta). Visualize x_t at selected indices, compute clean-signal and noise amplitudes; use oracle epsilon to reconstruct x0 and a perturbed epsilon to compare error. Explain that oracle is not a trained denoiser.

## Failure analysis
Confusing α_t and cumulative ᾱ_t mis-scales noise; check endpoints and use scheduler-consistent prediction parameterization.
