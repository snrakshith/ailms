# D02-L01 — Datasets, DataLoaders and training loops: worked solution

## Problem
Implement train_epoch and evaluate functions with sample-weighted losses.

## Worked approach
Build TensorDataset and DataLoader with 11 samples, batch 4. In train_epoch call zero_grad, forward, loss.backward, step; add loss.item()*len(y). Evaluate similarly without step under no_grad. Divide total by samples. Snapshot parameters before/after evaluate and assert equal.

## Failure analysis
Averaging three batch means equally weights the final three examples too much; sum weighted losses and divide by count.

## Acceptance check
Training changes weights; evaluation does not; batch sizes are handled correctly.
