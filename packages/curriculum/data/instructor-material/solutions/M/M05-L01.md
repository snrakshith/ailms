# M05-L01 — Time series, forecasting and backtesting: worked solution

## Problem
Build three expanding-window backtests and compare MAE to seasonal naive.

## Worked approach
Generate or load daily series; pick 3 cutoffs separated by horizon h=7. At each use only data through cutoff, predict next 7 using seasonal naive and a candidate such as moving average. Compute per-window MAE and mean, retaining all origins. Verify indices strictly follow train.

## Failure analysis
Random split permits future leakage; use ordered windows and avoid centered rolling features.

## Acceptance check
All prediction dates follow their training data; forecast horizon is constant.
