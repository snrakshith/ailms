# M02-L01 — Linear and polynomial regression: worked solution

## Problem
Fit degrees 1, 3 and 10 and compare validation RMSE.

## Worked approach
Generate x uniform [-1,1], y=1+2x−x²+noise. Split before fitting. For degrees 1,3,10 use PolynomialFeatures(include_bias=False), scaling and linear regression or ridge. Record train and validation RMSE, choose smallest validation error and assess test once; degree 3 should often suffice but results vary.

## Failure analysis
Selecting degree 10 from training error encourages overfit; plot validation error and investigate extrapolation outside training range.

## Acceptance check
Select the model by validation performance, not lowest training error.
