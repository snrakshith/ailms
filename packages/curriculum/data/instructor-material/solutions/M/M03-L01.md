# M03-L01 — Classification, regression and ranking metrics: worked solution

## Problem
Calculate confusion metrics and choose a metric for fraud review, demand forecast and search ranking.

## Worked approach
For TP=30, FP=20, FN=10, TN=940, precision=.6, recall=.75, F1=.667. For fraud use precision at review capacity plus recall; for demand MAE and asymmetric cost; for search NDCG@k when graded relevance matters. Report per-region or segment denominators.

## Failure analysis
Accuracy .97 masks rare-event errors; inspect confusion counts and business capacity rather than reporting accuracy alone.

## Acceptance check
Metric choice matches business costs and includes subgroup analysis.

## Submission evidence
Include implementation or design artifact, test inputs and outputs, relevant dependency versions, and a short limitation statement. For external tools, report skipped integrations explicitly; a mock is not a live service test.
