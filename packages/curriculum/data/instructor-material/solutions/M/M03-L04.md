# M03-L04 — Interpretability, fairness and error analysis: worked solution

## Problem

Produce an error slice report with subgroup sizes, metrics and limitations.

## Worked approach

Group predictions by relevant audited segment, compute support, false-negative rate and precision with denominator guards; mark small n as uncertain. Inspect error examples and feature provenance, then test one mitigation on validation. Document trade-offs and privacy constraints.

## Failure analysis

A subgroup with two examples has 100% error; report support and intervals, avoid broad claims or targeting based on unstable estimates.

## Acceptance check

Small groups are flagged; proposed mitigations are evaluated on validation data.

## Submission evidence

Include implementation or design artifact, test inputs and outputs, relevant dependency versions, and a short limitation statement. For external tools, report skipped integrations explicitly; a mock is not a live service test.

## Grading rubric (10 points)

- Correct mechanism or design: 4
- Evidence against acceptance criteria: 3
- Failure handling and boundary cases: 2
- Reproducibility and limitations: 1
