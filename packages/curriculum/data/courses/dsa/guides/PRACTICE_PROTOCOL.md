# Daily practice and spaced repetition

The source suggests 90–120 minutes daily: review the pattern, attempt problems before hints, explain the approach, code, analyze Big-O, log difficulties. For a beginner, spread six problems across extra sessions if necessary; 105 minutes is a suggested lesson time, not a guarantee of finishing every exercise.

| Status | Revisit |
|---|---|
| Green (independent) | End of week |
| Yellow (hint) | +2 days, end of week |
| Red (solution needed) | Next day, +3 days, end of week |

Take a two-hour review after each seven learning days. Retry Red items from memory; use 20 minutes for most initial attempts and 30 minutes for hard items. Keep a log with problem ID, initial approach, invariant, complexity, edge cases and next review.

## Interview ritual
Clarify input and constraints; state brute force; choose pattern; prove invariant; implement; trace examples and boundary cases; explain time and space. Company-specific days follow the PDF labels, not claims about current interviews.
