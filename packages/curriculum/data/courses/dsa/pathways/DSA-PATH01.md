# Interview preparation

Follow all days, keep the source practice log, complete mock interviews.

## Suggested sequence

1. Optional beginner prerequisites
2. Arrays & hashing
3. Two pointers & sliding window
4. Binary search
5. Stack & monotonic stack
6. Linked lists & caches
7. Trees & binary search trees
8. Tries & priority queues
9. Graph traversal and algorithms
10. Backtracking
11. DP foundations and grids
12. Advanced dynamic programming
13. Greedy and intervals
14. Bit manipulation & math
15. Interview sprint

Prerequisite check: complete a diagnostic on skipped topics. Exit: demonstrate a portfolio project, explain trade-offs, and pass the module checks.
