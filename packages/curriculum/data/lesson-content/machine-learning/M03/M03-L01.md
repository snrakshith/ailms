# Classification, regression and ranking metrics

**M03-L01 · Intermediate · 105 min including practice**

## Learning objective

Select metrics based on task and operating constraints.

## Concept notes

Precision measures positive prediction correctness; recall measures positive coverage. ROC-AUC evaluates ranking across thresholds, while PR curves highlight rare-positive trade-offs. MAE and RMSE measure regression errors; nDCG rewards relevant ranked results near the top.

## Worked scenario

Compare two fraud systems with equal accuracy but different minority recall.

## Common mistake

AUC does not identify an operating threshold and accuracy can hide minority failures.

## Practice exercise

Calculate confusion metrics and choose a metric for fraud review, demand forecast and search ranking.

## Deeper theory and design

Precision=TP/(TP+FP), recall=TP/(TP+FN), F1 harmonizes them. MAE is absolute error; RMSE emphasizes outliers. Ranking metrics such as recall@k and NDCG@k evaluate top results, not score calibration.

## Debugging clinic

Accuracy .97 masks rare-event errors; inspect confusion counts and business capacity rather than reporting accuracy alone.
