# Interpretability, fairness and error analysis

**M03-L04 · Advanced · 135 min including practice**

## Learning objective

Investigate failures and assess subgroup performance.

## Concept notes

Permutation importance measures score changes when a feature is shuffled; SHAP attributes model output relative to a baseline. Explanations are not causal evidence. Fairness metrics can conflict, so choose context-appropriate definitions and examine subgroups.

## Worked scenario

Compare recall and false-positive rate across relevant consented groups and investigate proxy features.

## Common mistake

Treating feature attribution as causality or reporting only aggregate accuracy.

## Practice exercise

Produce an error slice report with subgroup sizes, metrics and limitations.

**Acceptance criteria:** Small groups are flagged; proposed mitigations are evaluated on validation data.

## Deeper theory and design

Local attribution explains model output under a chosen feature perturbation/reference, not causal effect. Fairness metrics depend on task and protected-group definitions; equalized odds and calibration may conflict. Slice estimates need uncertainty and adequate sample sizes.

## Debugging clinic

A subgroup with two examples has 100% error; report support and intervals, avoid broad claims or targeting based on unstable estimates.
