# Anomaly detection and novelty detection

**M04-L03 · Intermediate · 105 min including practice**

## Learning objective

Identify unusual observations with scarce labels.

## Concept notes

Isolation Forest isolates observations via random splits; one-class methods estimate a normal region. Anomaly scores require thresholds and operational review; anomalies may be benign or reflect data quality.

## Worked scenario

Detect rare sensor spikes with a model trained on normal historical data.

## Common mistake

Training on future incidents or assuming every anomaly is malicious.

## Practice exercise

Evaluate precision at a fixed daily alert budget using labeled anomalies.

**Acceptance criteria:** Report false positives and sensitivity to changing baseline behavior.

## Deeper theory and design

Anomaly detection flags unusual observations relative to training distribution; novelty detection assumes relatively clean reference data. A score is not a probability of fraud. Fixed alert capacity is an operational constraint.

## Debugging clinic

Training on contaminated data or reversing score sign hides real anomalies. Inspect score direction and review labeled examples.
