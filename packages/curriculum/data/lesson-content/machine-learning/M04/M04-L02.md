# Dimensionality reduction: PCA, t-SNE and UMAP

**M04-L02 · Intermediate · 105 min including practice**

## Learning objective

Choose linear reduction versus neighborhood visualization.

## Concept notes

PCA is a linear projection; t-SNE and UMAP construct nonlinear embeddings emphasizing aspects of neighborhood structure. Low-dimensional plots may distort global distances, density and cluster separation.

## Worked scenario

Reduce handwritten digit vectors to two dimensions and compare stability across seeds.

## Common mistake

Interpreting all distances and cluster sizes in t-SNE as meaningful.

## Practice exercise

Compare PCA explained variance and multiple nonlinear embeddings.

## Deeper theory and design

PCA gives orthogonal linear directions of maximum variance. t-SNE emphasizes local neighborhoods and may distort global distances; UMAP approximates a neighborhood graph embedding. Neither nonlinear visualization alone validates cluster labels.

## Debugging clinic

Interpreting distances between t-SNE islands as real distances is unjustified; check original-space neighbors and repeat seeds.
