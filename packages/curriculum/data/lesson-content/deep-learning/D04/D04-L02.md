# Autoencoders, VAEs and GANs

**D04-L02 · Advanced · 135 min including practice**

## Learning objective

Compare reconstruction and generative modeling objectives.

## Concept notes

Autoencoders reconstruct inputs through a latent representation; VAEs learn probabilistic latents with reconstruction and KL terms. GANs train a generator against a discriminator in an adversarial objective.

## Worked scenario

Train a small autoencoder and inspect reconstruction error and latent structure.

## Common mistake

A good reconstruction score does not guarantee useful generation; GANs may collapse modes.

## Practice exercise

## Deeper theory and design

Autoencoders reconstruct input through bottleneck. VAE learns a distribution q(z given x), samples via reparameterization and balances reconstruction with KL regularization. GAN uses competing generator/discriminator objectives, not a reconstruction requirement.

## Debugging clinic

Good reconstruction does not ensure useful generation or anomaly detection; validate shift cases and avoid interpreting reconstruction score as calibrated probability.
