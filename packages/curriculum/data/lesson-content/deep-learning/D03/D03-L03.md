# Attention and transformers

**D03-L03 · Intermediate · 105 min including practice**

## Learning objective

Explain queries, keys, values, masking and positional information.

## Concept notes

Scaled dot-product attention computes softmax(QK^T/sqrt(d))V. Multihead attention captures different projections. Transformers combine attention, feedforward layers, residuals, normalization and position information; causal masks prevent attending to future tokens.

## Worked scenario

Compute attention weights for three tokens and compare masked versus unmasked output.

## Common mistake

Mask polarity errors and quadratic attention memory for long sequences.

## Practice exercise

## Deeper theory and design

Scaled dot-product attention computes softmax(QKᵀ/√d)V. Causal mask assigns −∞ to future positions before softmax. Attention's quadratic sequence-length cost is a major long-context bottleneck (implementation optimizations may reduce memory).

## Debugging clinic

Applying mask after softmax leaves incorrect normalization; mask scores before softmax, avoid fully masked rows or define safe handling.
