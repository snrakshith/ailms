# Encoder, decoder and encoder-decoder architectures

**D03-L04 · Advanced · 135 min including practice**

## Learning objective
Choose architecture for classification, generation and sequence translation.

## Concept notes
Encoders typically use bidirectional context for representations; decoder-only language models use causal attention for next-token generation; encoder-decoder models condition decoding on encoded input. Architecture choice affects masking and training objectives.

## Worked scenario
Compare BERT-like classification, GPT-like generation and T5-like summarization.

## Common mistake
Assuming every transformer has identical masking or can generate text autoregressively.

## Deeper theory and design

Encoder self-attention typically sees all source positions; decoder-only autoregressive models mask future tokens; encoder-decoder combines bidirectional source encoding, causal target self-attention and cross-attention to source. Training objectives differ.

## Debugging clinic

Allowing target positions to attend to future target tokens during training creates leakage; use causal target mask.
