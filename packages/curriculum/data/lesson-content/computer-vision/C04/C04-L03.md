# OCR, document AI, 3D and deployment

**C04-L03 · Advanced · 135 min including practice**

## Learning objective

Identify OCR pipelines, depth estimation and efficient vision inference.

## Concept notes

OCR often combines text detection and recognition; document AI adds layout and structure. Depth can come from stereo geometry or learned models. Deployment uses batching, quantization and export formats such as ONNX; preprocessing must match training.

## Worked scenario

Extract text from synthetic receipts and benchmark an exported classifier.

## Common mistake

Logging sensitive documents or ignoring coordinate transforms during resizing.
