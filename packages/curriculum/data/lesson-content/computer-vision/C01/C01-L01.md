# Image representation, color spaces and OpenCV

**C01-L01 · Beginner · 75 min including practice**

## Learning objective
Read, inspect and convert images correctly.

## Concept notes
Images are arrays of pixels with spatial dimensions and channels. OpenCV commonly reads color images in BGR order; RGB, grayscale and HSV serve different tasks. Data range and dtype affect arithmetic.

## Worked scenario
Convert a BGR image to grayscale and HSV; inspect channel histograms.

## Common mistake
Displaying BGR as RGB or overflowing uint8 during arithmetic.

## Practice exercise
Create a synthetic color image, convert spaces and verify channel values.

## Deeper theory and design

An image is an array with spatial and channel dimensions; dtype determines representable range. OpenCV commonly uses BGR, while many plotting/model tools expect RGB. HSV separates hue from saturation/value but hue wraparound matters.

## Debugging clinic

Displaying BGR as RGB swaps red and blue. Convert at library boundaries and document normalization ranges.
