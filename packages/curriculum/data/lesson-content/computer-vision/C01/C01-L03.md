# Geometric transforms, calibration and features

**C01-L03 · Intermediate · 105 min including practice**

## Learning objective
Estimate mappings and match local image structures.

## Concept notes
Affine transforms preserve parallelism; homographies model planar projective mappings. Camera intrinsics and distortion parameters support calibration. ORB detects and describes local features for matching.

## Worked scenario
Match features between a synthetic image and a rotated version, then estimate a homography with RANSAC.

## Common mistake
Homographies fail for many nonplanar scenes; feature matches can contain outliers.

## Practice exercise
Apply a perspective transform and quantify inlier matches or reprojection error.

## Deeper theory and design

Affine transforms preserve parallelism; homographies model perspective mapping between planes. Feature matching pairs descriptors, and RANSAC estimates geometry robustly under outliers. Reprojection error compares transformed points with observed correspondences.

## Debugging clinic

A homography fitted to four points exactly may be wrong for nonplanar scenes; inspect additional correspondences and camera geometry.
