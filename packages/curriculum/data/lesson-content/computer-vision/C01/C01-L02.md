# Filtering, edges and morphology

**C01-L02 · Beginner · 75 min including practice**

## Learning objective
Apply smoothing, gradients, thresholding and morphological operators.

## Concept notes
Convolution filters alter local neighborhoods. Gaussian blur reduces high-frequency noise; Sobel and Canny detect edges; erosion and dilation change binary regions. Opening removes small foreground structures, closing fills small gaps.

## Worked scenario
Clean a noisy binary mask and detect edges around a shape.

## Common mistake
Over-smoothing removes important detail; morphology depends on kernel and polarity.

## Practice exercise
Compare masks for multiple kernel sizes and quantify foreground area.

**Acceptance criteria:** Preserve target shape while removing isolated noise.

## Deeper theory and design

Convolutional filtering smooths or emphasizes local variation; Canny detects edges after smoothing/gradient processing. Morphological erosion shrinks foreground, dilation expands it; opening removes isolated bright pixels, closing fills small gaps.

## Debugging clinic

Large erosion removes thin defects; evaluate object retention and noise reduction against annotated examples.
