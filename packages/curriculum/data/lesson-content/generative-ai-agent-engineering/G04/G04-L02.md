# Corrective RAG (CRAG)

**G04-L02 · Advanced · 135 min including practice**

## Learning objective
Use retrieval-quality assessment to trigger corrective retrieval.

## Concept notes
The CRAG research approach evaluates retrieved-document quality and triggers corrective actions, including knowledge refinement and possible external search. An application-inspired implementation may route to rewrite, alternate index or approved search when evidence is weak.

## Worked scenario
Grade retrieval as strong, weak or ambiguous; route weak cases to a second trusted corpus.

## Common mistake
Sending sensitive queries to unapproved search or using corrective results without provenance.

## Practice exercise
Implement a three-way retrieval-quality router with an offline fallback corpus.

**Acceptance criteria:** Route tests cover strong, weak, ambiguous and unavailable fallback cases.

## Deeper theory and design

CRAG assesses retrieval quality and selects corrective actions, including knowledge refinement or external search in the research approach. A three-way router can represent strong, ambiguous and weak evidence; thresholds should be calibrated rather than treated as universally valid. External retrieval expands the trust boundary.

## Debugging clinic

A fallback leaks confidential queries to an external service or inserts stale documents. Authorize sources and redact/deny queries before retrieval; retain provenance.
