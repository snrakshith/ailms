# Self-corrective RAG and iterative verification

**G04-L03 · Advanced · 135 min including practice**

## Learning objective
Design bounded correction of unsupported or incomplete answers.

## Concept notes
Self-corrective RAG is a broad application pattern rather than a single universal algorithm. Verify claims against evidence, revise or retrieve again and stop when quality gates pass or budget is exhausted.

## Worked scenario
Detect missing citation support in a generated answer and regenerate only unsupported claims.

## Common mistake
Infinite loops and a judge repeatedly approving its own hallucination.

## Practice exercise
Implement a verifier with max three attempts and an explicit abstain state.

**Acceptance criteria:** All runs terminate; unsupported claims are removed or flagged.

## Deeper theory and design

Self-corrective RAG verifies generated claims, not just retrieval quality. Correction may revise unsupported claims, retrieve missing evidence or abstain. Iteration needs a bounded budget and a progress signal; repeated same evidence is not progress.

## Debugging clinic

An unbounded verifier loop increases cost while never resolving a missing document. Add max attempts, evidence-change check and terminal failure state.
