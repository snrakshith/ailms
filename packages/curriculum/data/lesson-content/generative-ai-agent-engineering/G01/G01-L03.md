# Decoding, sampling and inference constraints

**G01-L03 · Intermediate · 105 min including practice**

## Learning objective
Compare greedy, temperature, top-k and top-p decoding.

## Concept notes
Greedy chooses a highest-probability token; temperature rescales logits; top-k limits candidate count; top-p retains a probability mass. Stops, output limits and constrained decoding influence completion behavior. Determinism is not guaranteed across serving systems.

## Worked scenario
Generate several responses under greedy and sampled settings and compare diversity.

## Common mistake
Using low temperature as a correctness guarantee or confusing top-p with top-k.

## Practice exercise
Record five generations per setting and compare format compliance and diversity.

## Deeper theory and design

Temperature rescales logits, top-k truncates candidates by rank and top-p keeps a cumulative probability mass. Greedy decoding selects highest score; low temperature may reduce variation but does not guarantee reproducibility across provider revisions or hardware. Sampling controls cannot guarantee truthful output.

## Debugging clinic

A consistently wrong answer looks reproducible; evaluate correctness and schema compliance, not just identical outputs.
