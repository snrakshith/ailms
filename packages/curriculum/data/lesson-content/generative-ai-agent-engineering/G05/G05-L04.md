# Multi-agent systems and orchestration

**G05-L04 · Advanced · 135 min including practice**

## Learning objective
Choose supervisor-worker, handoff and parallel specialist patterns.

## Concept notes
Multiple agents can divide tasks or specialize tools; a supervisor coordinates routing and state, while handoff transfers responsibility. Coordination costs and inconsistent state can outweigh benefits. Shared contracts and permissions are necessary.

## Worked scenario
A research coordinator delegates retrieval and verification to separate workers then merges evidence.

## Common mistake
Recursive delegation, conflicting writes and sharing unnecessary private context.

## Practice exercise

## Deeper theory and design

Multi-agent systems partition responsibility among workers, often supervisor, specialists or peer roles. Shared state needs conflict-resolution and ownership rules; fan-out increases latency/cost and may propagate errors. Independent verification is preferable to agent consensus alone.

## Debugging clinic

Giving each specialist every tool defeats least privilege. Restrict capabilities per worker and validate merged provenance.
