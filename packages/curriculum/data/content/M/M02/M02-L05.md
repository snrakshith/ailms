## Concept notes
Boosting builds an additive model by fitting successive learners to loss gradients or related targets. Learning rate, tree complexity and iterations trade bias, variance and computation. Libraries differ in categorical handling and histogram strategies.

## Worked scenario
Train a histogram gradient boosting baseline before trying external libraries.

## Common mistake

## Deeper theory and design

Boosting sequentially fits learners to gradients of a loss, so each stage corrects ensemble errors. Learning rate, iteration count and tree depth interact. Histogram boosting bins features; external XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost differ in categorical support and implementation.
