# Imbalanced learning, calibration and uncertainty

**M03-L03 · Advanced · 135 min including practice**

## Learning objective
Evaluate probabilities and handle rare classes responsibly.

## Concept notes
Class weights alter training loss; resampling changes the training distribution. Calibration compares predicted probability with observed frequency and can use held-out sigmoid or isotonic fitting. Prediction intervals or conformal methods quantify uncertainty under stated assumptions.

## Worked scenario
Use class weighting for rare events, then compare reliability diagrams before and after calibration.

## Common mistake
Oversampling before splitting leaks duplicate or related rows; calibration requires separate data or folds.

## Practice exercise
Compare precision-recall, Brier score and calibration curves on a natural-prevalence holdout.

## Deeper theory and design

Ranking quality differs from calibration: a calibrated score of .2 should correspond to roughly 20% positives in comparable examples. Brier score averages squared probability error. Resampling changes training prevalence and may distort probabilities.

## Debugging clinic

Oversampling before split duplicates positives into holdout; resample inside training only and do not change test prevalence.
